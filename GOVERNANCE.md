# Governance

ArchIsland is maintained by its owner, [@YCistak](https://github.com/YCistak),
who has final say on direction, releases and merges.

## How decisions are made

- Bugs and concrete feature proposals are discussed in
  [issues](https://github.com/YCistak/archisland/issues).
- Open-ended ideas and questions belong in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/YCistak/archisland/discussions).
- Every change, including the maintainer's own, goes through a pull request into
  `main`. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- The maintainer aims to reach consensus in the thread first; when that is not
  possible the maintainer decides and explains the reasoning.

## Becoming a collaborator

Contributors who have landed several quality pull requests, follow the
[Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and show good judgment in review may be
invited as collaborators. Open a discussion or contact the maintainer if you are
interested. Collaborator access can be removed at the maintainer's discretion.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance are proposed through a pull request and decided by the
maintainer.
